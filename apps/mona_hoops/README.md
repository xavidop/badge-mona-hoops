# Mona Hoops

A sixty-second basketball shootout: charge the shot, pick the arc and sink it from spots that keep moving back.

Hold Select to charge the swinging power meter and release to shoot; Up and
Down change the arc. Far spots are worth three, swishes earn a bonus, and
three in a row puts the ball on fire for extra points.

## Controls

* Select: hold to charge, release to shoot; start the game; play again
* Up / Down: raise or lower the arc
* Back: back to the title

## Requirements

No network. Best score and total makes are saved under `/state`. The sprites
in `assets/` are drawn by the repository's `tools/build_sprites.py`.
